# AI Future Scope

## Purpose

AI is optional and must not be introduced before deterministic platform foundations are reliable.

## Candidate Uses

- trade ranking
- anomaly detection
- parameter optimization
- regime classification
- strategy research assistant
- report generation

## Constraints

- AI must not bypass risk rules.
- AI outputs must be auditable.
- AI should assist decisions before it automates decisions.
- Deterministic fallbacks are required for execution-critical flows.
