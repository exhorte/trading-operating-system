# Decision Process

Use ADRs for decisions that affect architecture, trading safety, persistence, execution, data contracts, or long-term maintainability.

## ADR Required When

- choosing a major technology
- defining a bounded context
- changing the execution architecture
- changing risk policy behavior
- adding a new broker connector
- changing data storage strategy
- replacing a major library

## Decision Template

- Context
- Decision
- Alternatives Considered
- Consequences
- Follow-Up
