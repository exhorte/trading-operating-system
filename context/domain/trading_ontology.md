# Trading Ontology

## Core Concepts

Symbol:

- tradable market such as XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, BTCUSD, DXY proxy.

Account:

- broker or prop firm account with balance, equity, leverage, drawdown limits, and execution permissions.

Position:

- open market exposure with entry, volume, SL, TP, floating P/L, and lifecycle events.

Order:

- instruction sent to an execution venue.

Trade:

- completed or partially completed position lifecycle with result and attribution.

Market Context:

- structured analysis snapshot including trend, bias, liquidity, sessions, news, risk, and signal quality.

Execution Command:

- server-approved instruction sent to an execution agent.

Execution Report:

- response from execution agent including accepted, rejected, filled, modified, closed, or failed.
